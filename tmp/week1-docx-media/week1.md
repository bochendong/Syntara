**Left-hand Sum and Right-hand Sum**$
$$$Left\ hand\ Sum:\ L_{n} = \sum_{i = 0}^{n - 1}{f\left( t_{i} \right)\Delta t} = f\left( t_{0} \right)\Delta t + f\left( t_{1} \right)\Delta t + \ldots + f\left( t_{n - 1} \right)\Delta t$$

$$Right\ hand\ Sum:\ L_{n}R_{n} = \sum_{i = 1}^{n}{f\left( t_{i} \right)\Delta t} = f\left( t_{1} \right)\Delta t + f\left( t_{1} \right)\Delta t + \ldots + f\left( t_{n} \right)\Delta t$$

**Chain Rule**

$$F(x) = \int_{u(x)}^{v(x)}{f(t)dt}$$

$$F^{'}(x) = f\left( v(x) \right)v^{'}(x) - f\left( u(x) \right)u^{'}(x)\ $$
